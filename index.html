Your complete workflow

How to make AI videos

Follow these steps from the first idea to publishing your finished video.

Open Google Flow ↗
💡
STEP 01

Choose your idea and write the script

Open ChatGPT, describe your topic and audience, then ask for a short script with a strong opening hook, scene-by-scene narration, and a memorable ending.

Open ChatGPT ↗
✍️
STEP 02

Prepare detailed AI prompts

Ask ChatGPT to turn every script scene into a visual prompt. Include the subject, action, setting, camera movement, lighting, mood, aspect ratio (9:16 for Shorts/Reels) and consistency notes.

Use Prompt Lab ↗
🎬
STEP 03

Generate scenes in Google Flow AI

Open Flow, start a project, choose a video workflow/model available to your account, paste one scene prompt, set the aspect ratio and generate. Review the clip and refine the prompt if needed.

Open Google Flow ↗
🧪
STEP 04

Try other AI video websites

If Flow is not available or you want another look, test tools such as Runway, Pika, Luma or Kling. Check each service's current access, credits, pricing and usage terms before creating.

Browse AI tools ↗
✂️
STEP 05

Edit, add voice and sound

Import your generated clips into CapCut for trimming, transitions, captions, music and sound effects. For advanced timeline editing use Adobe Premiere; use After Effects for motion graphics and visual effects.

CapCut ↗Premiere ↗After Effects ↗
🚀
STEP 06

Export and publish

Watch the full edit, check captions and audio, export in a format supported by your platform, then upload to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels or your chosen social network. Add a clear title, relevant description and accurate labels.

YouTube Studio ↗Instagram ↗
COPY-READY CHATGPT REQUEST

Generate a complete AI video plan

Copy this into ChatGPT and replace the topic with your own idea.

Create a complete AI video plan about [YOUR TOPIC] for a 30–60 second vertical 9:16 YouTube Short / Instagram Reel. Give me: (1) a powerful 2-second hook, (2) a concise voiceover script, (3) a scene-by-scene storyboard with approximate timings, (4) a separate detailed Google Flow AI prompt for each scene including subject, action, environment, camera movement, lighting, mood and visual consistency, (5) voiceover and sound-effect suggestions, (6) an editing checklist for CapCut, Adobe Premiere and After Effects, and (7) a title, description and relevant hashtags. Keep all scenes visually consistent and avoid putting text inside generated footage unless requested.

Creator tip: AI tool availability, free credits, watermarks and commercial-use permissions can change. Check the terms for each tool and use music, voices and assets you have permission to publish.

Learning paths

Lesson 1 — Prompt engineering basics

A useful prompt states the goal, context, subject, constraints, output format and quality criteria.

  • Say exactly what you want to make.
  • Describe the audience and style.
  • Specify length, format and restrictions.
  • Review the result and improve one detail at a time.
Find tutorials ↗
Lesson 2 — AI video production

Write a script, divide it into scenes, generate clips, edit sound and captions, then export for the target platform.

Find tutorials ↗
Lesson 3 — Editing fundamentals

Learn trimming, pacing, continuity, audio levels, subtitles, transitions and export settings. Use effects to support the story, not distract from it.

Find tutorials ↗
Lesson 4 — Freelance readiness

Build 3–5 strong samples, define deliverables and revision limits, agree on price and deadline in writing, and ask clients for clear references.
